- Reading A, constructive: bias falls monotonically as the cap loosens except seed 0 at caps [0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0]; seed 1 at caps [0.5, 1.0]; seed 2 at caps [0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.5]; seed 3 at caps [0.5, 1.0]; seed 4 at caps [0.5, 0.75, 1.5, 2.0].
- Reading A, variational: bias falls monotonically as the cap loosens except seed 2 at caps [2.0].
- Reading B, constructive: bias falls monotonically as the cap loosens for every seed.
- Reading B, variational: bias falls monotonically as the cap loosens except seed 0 at caps [1.5]; seed 1 at caps [1.5, 2.0]; seed 2 at caps [2.0]; seed 3 at caps [1.5]; seed 4 at caps [1.5].
- Variational bias is lower than constructive in 90 of 90 matched chains.
- The paper reports bias 0.46 at J_max = 0.3 and 0.024 at J_max = 10, settling within about 3 sweeps, and bias ≈ 0.6 × its plotted floor. Its instance is unpublished, so only trends and orders of magnitude are comparable.

floor_D = η_sweep/(1 − ρ_D) is the only proven bound. floor_paper = ε̄/(1 − SLEM) reproduces the paper's plotted comparison and is not a guaranteed bound.
