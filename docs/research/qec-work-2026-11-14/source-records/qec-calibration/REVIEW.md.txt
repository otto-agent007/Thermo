# Independent review of exact-fixture reliability investigation

## Verification

`independent_audit.py` independently enumerates all 4,096 physical fault vectors
per fixture, filters them by syndrome, and sums direct Bernoulli probability
products. It does not use the production affine mass oracle. It verifies the
frozen plan hash, 768 saved AIS weight arrays and ESS values, all 192 group
statistics, seed-mass bounds, candidate accept/reject counts, per-fixture exact
conditional binomial intervals, and the selected-threshold result.

Audit passed. **No candidate qualifies under the predeclared selection rule.**
The strictest candidate accepts 32 calibration groups, including five inaccurate
ones; on validation it accepts 28, including one inaccurate one. These pooled
counts describe the fixed heterogeneous fixtures, not an iid population rate.

`replay_coverage.py` additionally independently replays two illustrative groups
with direct recomputation of physical-state target probabilities at each move.
All eight retained weight arrays and their visited-mass totals agree:

- Calibration 601/top4/group1 visits states carrying 99.9946% of exact target mass,
  yet estimates total mass 19.3734% too low and passes the strictest heuristic.
- Validation 701/top4/group2 visits states carrying 99.9996% of exact target mass,
  yet estimates total mass 15.2294% too high and passes that heuristic.

Thus merely visiting important states is insufficient: occupation, trajectory
weights, and variability still matter. These results do not prove an algebraic
bias or a failure of AIS in general.

## Statistical interpretation

Four-replicate Student-t intervals are heuristic with these potentially skewed,
heavy-tailed positive estimates. Their nominal 95% label is not a coverage
certificate. Exact-fixture checks appropriately falsified the candidate rules;
no parameter should be retuned against validation outcomes and presented as
validated on those same outcomes.

A point estimate below a known mass lower bound can be ordinary finite-sampling
fluctuation. It is not a deterministic impossibility or, by itself, evidence of
an implementation defect. Comparing the heuristic interval to the bound still
does not establish accuracy. High ESS also does not certify correct mass.

The omissions remove four highest-probability configurations, not necessarily
an entire distinct mode or basin. Actual seed target-mass fractions are saved.
The exact visited-mass measurement is an oracle diagnostic only, not a practical
d5 acceptance feature. Per-fixture uncertainty is broad with only 16 groups.

## Next decision

Do not deploy or expand efficacy tests using these reliability gates. A separate
predeclared investigation could analytically sum known seed mass and estimate
only the residual, verifying the exact weighted-endpoint identity first. That
would respect the lower bound structurally without clipping, but would not by
itself guarantee lower variance, missing-mode detection, or valid confidence
intervals. No such new method was implemented or run in this review.
