## Prespecified diagnostic

Pooled heterogeneous counts above are summaries, NOT iid-binomial population estimates. results.json provides per-fixture conditional false-accept counts and exact95% intervals; with at most16groups per fixture uncertainty is substantial even when zero errors occur.

The nominal95% Student-t interval's pooled descriptive empirical coverage was89/96(92.7%) calibration and91/96(94.8%) validation. This does not validate95% coverage for d5 or other fixtures. Four heavy-tailed AIS replicates can underestimate uncertainty; ESS and known-seed bounds do not fix that.

Visited states carried nearly all exact target mass for most groups/fixtures (mean about98.9%–100% by fixture). Nonetheless mass estimates could err by up to39%. Visiting important states sometime during annealing is insufficient: path weights and frequency must also be accurate. The current small fibers are easier to traverse than d5; high visited coverage does not establish adequate d5 mode coverage. Excluding the top4 seeds is not necessarily excluding an entire energetic basin, especially with tied energies.

## Consequence
Do not advance this gate or expand d5 efficacy. The revised proposal corrects the earlier catastrophic origin problem but practical reliability remains unresolved. Next design options include seed-mass plus separately estimated residual mass, richer independent replications and explicitly calibrated uncertainty on exact difficult fixtures. A seed-plus-residual estimator must be derived/tested, not clipping an estimate upward to its lower bound. No such additional arm was run here.

Sources, full particle logweights, exact fixture definitions, fixed plan, replay checks and independent review are bundled. Wall time10.08seconds. Tests validate instrumentation against original estimator and a deliberately false-confidence fixture; they do not turn a rejected statistical rule into a passing one.
