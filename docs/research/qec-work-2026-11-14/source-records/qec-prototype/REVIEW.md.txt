# Independent mathematical review

## Scope and result

Reviewed the development prototype only, without modifying the frozen pilot.
Six independent tests added in `test_exact_independent.py`; aggregate prototype
suite at review time: **10 passed**. No broader decoding efficacy study was run
by this reviewer.

The proposal is a normalized mixture over free coordinates of the affine fiber.
Each center is validated against that fiber, duplicate fault configurations are
removed, and the full overlapping mixture density (including uniform background)
is used in both AIS weights and transitions. The mixture is fixed before retained
AIS trajectories. Its positive background supplies mathematical full support,
not a practical exploration guarantee in high dimensions.

The implementation independently samples each particle's proposed coordinate and
acceptance draw. Single-coordinate proposals are symmetric. The acceptance ratio
therefore correctly uses beta times the target log-density difference plus
(1-beta) times the complete proposal log-density difference. Weighting precedes
transition at every temperature. The known mass bound sums probabilities of
unique valid configurations only.

## Independent evidence

- A 9-fault affine fixture has 32 feasible states and two dominant low-weight
  modes separated by three free-coordinate flips. Neither mode equals the
  all-zero elimination offset. Direct probability products supply exact mass.
- The full mixture normalizes, has positive support, and places substantial
  mass on each of the two separated modes.
- Exhaustive state-transition propagation verifies detailed balance and the
  AIS expected-weight identity. This test validates the intended mathematical
  kernel, independently of Monte Carlo fluctuation.
- Actual no-transition AIS weights match direct target products divided by the
  full mixture density.
- A deterministic independent-particle replay recomputes target probabilities
  from fault vectors at each proposal and agrees with production weights. This
  would catch a return to common-coordinate particle proposals or erroneous
  incremental density updates.
- A fixed-seed synthetic test agrees with exact total mass within its declared
  5% tolerance. This is a regression check, not a statistical convergence claim.

## Limits and safeguards

The lower-bound check is a one-sided alarm. A finite unbiased estimator can fall
below a valid bound by sampling fluctuation; rejecting it is conservative, not
proof of a software bug. Passing the bound provides no upper error bound, no
certificate that important modes were covered, and no logical-class ordering
certificate. Do not clamp an estimate up to the bound and treat it as validated.

Likewise, ESS measures weight concentration, not correctness. Independent
particles remove one source of dependence but cannot turn high ESS into a
coverage guarantee. Distinct low-energy seeds need not belong to distinct modes.
Seed selection/MILP cost must be included in future end-to-end cost comparisons.

The harmful pilot case is now development data. Improving its answer is useful
for debugging, but it cannot establish general superiority, exact class-mass
accuracy, or held-out performance. Any revised efficacy comparison needs an
explicitly frozen configuration and genuinely held-out saved indices.
