# Independent review of the sliced contraction error envelope

## Decision

**Accept the proposed conservative absolute envelope conditionally on the stated
correctly-rounded longdouble arithmetic assumptions.** This is forward-error
analysis of this fixed implementation/model, not a hardware-level formal proof,
a statistical interval, or a relative-accuracy certificate for the tiny class1
mass. Keep the assumptions alongside any reported enclosure.

## Checked argument

I inspected `tensor_slice_run.py`, `tensor_full_slices.py`, the symbolic depth
script and saved model. Each complete fault contributes a Fourier factor either
1 or 1-2p; here p<=0.008300000000000026, so these factors are positive and at least
0.9834. The local expression (1-p)+p*(+/-1) is well-conditioned: four conservative
rounding units per initial factor cover its construction. Binary64 probabilities
convert exactly to this platform's 64-significand-bit longdouble. Unary factors
+/-1/2 are exact.

Every original factor is consumed exactly once by a bucket tree. Expanding that
arithmetic circuit gives one monomial per complete assignment to the121Fourier
variables. Replacing signed unaries by their absolute values bounds the sum of
absolute exact monomials by1: there are2^121assignments, each has121factors1/2,
and each fault factor lies in[0,1]. This controls INTERNAL cancellation as well
as the final slice sum. It is not inferred from the observed slice-sum condition
number.

The recorded symbolic depth2332per slice and2588including a sequential256-term
sum is consistent with summing child depths, charging each product, and one
addition per two-entry eliminated axis. NumPy may use a different reduction
order, but its binary sums need no greater sequential depth. Even allowing an
extra addition per eliminated-axis reduction, decimal serialization/reparse,
and final sign handling leaves substantial room below8192. Serialization must
remain a faithful round-to-nearest representation; a conservative extra conversion
rounding is covered rather than assuming perfect roundtrip.

Use epsilon=2^-63 (NumPy longdouble eps), conservatively twice conventional unit
roundoff, and delta=8192. For normal operations, absolute error is bounded by

  gamma_delta = (delta*epsilon)/(1-delta*epsilon).

The argument does not assume that intermediate signed results have small relative
error with respect to the final answer. Each elementary addition is expanded into
the original signed monomials, and their absolute sum supplies the envelope.

## Range and underflow allowance

There are at most518original factors,113eliminations per slice and256slices.
The largest sliced bucket has2^24entries. Charging every possible factor-consumption
multiplication and eliminated-axis addition at that largest size gives fewer
than2^43elementary arithmetic operations, including setup and aggregation slack.
An intermediate exact expanded magnitude is at most2^121, far below the
longdouble overflow threshold; the small rounding allowance does not alter this.

For underflow, charge an absolute error of the normal minimum at every operation,
even though this is much larger than a correctly-rounded subnormal error. Each
such perturbation's remaining algebraic amplification is bounded conservatively
by2^129: the rest of the Fourier sum has at most2^121assignments and bounded
positive factors, with ample rounding slack. The aggregate is then less than
2^43*2^129*normal_minimum, below1e-4880 on this platform. Adding1e-4800 is therefore
more than sufficient. This relies on the finite arithmetic/range assumptions;
unchecked arbitrary compiler or hardware behavior is not certified.

## Endpoint construction and meaning

Form delta and epsilon as exact rationals, add the conservative underflow floor,
and evaluate displayed endpoints exactly or round them outward. Do not obtain a
certificate by rounding an error bar inward in binary64.

The resulting roughly8.88e-16absolute envelope is large relative to the reported
~1.4e-20class1 value. It cannot validate that tiny number's relative accuracy.
It may nevertheless separate the two logical classes and give useful conservative
class0candidate-coverage bounds. Those decisions must be made from the final
outward endpoints, incorporating existing exact candidate lower bounds.
