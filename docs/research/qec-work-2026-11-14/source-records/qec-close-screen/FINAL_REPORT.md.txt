# Thermo held-out close-case validation, 14 November 2026

## Bottom line
The study completed all 14 frozen reference cases with no execution crashes, no retries, and no dropped cases. It found no advantage over Tesseract. The four-candidate policy repaired some belief-matching decisions in an enriched disagreement sample, but neither corrected BP scores nor short-list mass gaps are established reliable ambiguity detectors.

## Root cause fixed
The earlier screen rejected any nonfinite BP LLR globally. Across all 1,536 development shots, every infinity was a valid degree-one hard constraint and none occurred on the logical observable; there were no NaNs. Rejecting these was a screening bug, not BP instability. The corrected independent-marginal parity score passes exact-enumeration tests but is not a calibrated logical posterior.

## Prespecified held-out design
Development: saved indices 0–288. Fresh shots: 289–800 inclusive in six X/Z × p=.005/.010/.020 cells, 3,072 total. Outcome labels were not accessed during screen, list scoring, or selection. Deterministic hash ranks fixed 32 random shots per cell and one reserved control before scoring. At most two hash-ranked decoder disagreements per cell were admitted, retaining/reporting excess. There were eight disagreements in the full screen; four admitted extras plus 192 random shots made the 196-shot list panel.

Lists contained at most four distinct feasible configurations per logical class, with fixed two-second MILP solve limits and explicit optimality checks. 138 panel shots yielded valid list scores; 58 were unknown and retained belief-matching fallback. Repeated shots retain multiplicity; 24/196 shared a syndrome with development data, so these are held-out shots, not exclusively novel syndromes.

Fourteen reference cases were selected without labels: six controls, six minimum valid list-margin cases excluding previously selected syndromes, and two disagreement cases. Missing strata were not replaced. Five low/noise list-close references were decisively outside the predeclared ambiguity region; the remaining list-close reference was numerically unresolved.

## What this supports next
Do not scale this into an efficacy claim or call the list4 screen calibrated. The useful research direction is to quantify omitted logical-class mass and improve conditional numerical bounds on the difficult cases, then validate any revised method on a genuinely new prespecified sample against Tesseract. That is a recommendation, not authorization or a launched follow-up experiment.
