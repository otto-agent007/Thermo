# Independent review: exact-seed plus AIS residual

## Mathematical result

The implementation uses the correct **final endpoint** indicator, not whether a
particle ever visited a seed. For a fixed normalized proposal, fixed seed set,
and invariant intermediate kernels, the AIS identity is

E[W 1{X_final=x}] = f(x).

Thus the estimator Z_seed + mean(W 1{X_final outside seeds}) is unbiased for total
mass. It is always at least Z_seed because its sampled residual is nonnegative.
This is a different estimator, not clipping an ordinary estimate to a bound.
The exact seed contribution sums unique valid fault assignments.

Four independent tests in `test_endpoint_independent.py` passed:
1. Exhaustive propagation checks the entire weighted terminal-state vector,
   then the residual expectation identity, on a finite affine fixture.
2. Independent direct-probability trajectory replay matches production endpoint
   states, weights and residual with no moves.
3. The same independent replay matches with three moves per temperature.
4. An exact counterexample establishes that residual variance can increase.
   With a proposal equal to the target, ordinary weights are constant; removing
   the known random seed contribution makes the residual estimator nonconstant.

## Saved-result audit

`independent_audit.py` verified both plan/source hashes, all **768** saved paired
weight arrays, final-state syndrome consistency, seed membership, nonseed
counts, ordinary and residual masses, structural bounds, and reported metrics.
Exact mass was independently recomputed by enumerating all 4,096 physical fault
vectors and summing direct Bernoulli products over the appropriate syndrome.

The empirical effect is mixed, not a blanket improvement:
- Historical validation701/top4: group relative MSE improves from 0.0110459 to
  3.03e-10, where the known seeds already account for almost all target mass.
- Its omitted-top4 counterpart changes from 0.0396111 to 0.0396179; both have
  9 of 16 group means more than 10% from exact mass.
- Several other fixtures worsen in relative MSE despite some improvements
  elsewhere. These finite sample differences are not proofs of population MSE.

The names "calibration" and "validation" are inherited fixture labels. The
residual investigation reuses already inspected fixtures and is a development
comparison, not new held-out validation.

## What this does not establish

The structural lower bound does not certify approximation accuracy. Unseen
important residual modes can still be missed. A run with no nonseed endpoints
returns exactly the known seed mass; that is not evidence the residual is zero.
No calibrated confidence interval follows from zero residual observations,
high ESS, or repeated agreement.

Known contribution removal does not guarantee lower total variance: covariance
with the residual contribution matters. Exact-fixture empirical bias is sampling
error, not evidence of asymptotic bias. No d5 efficacy conclusion or authorization
for an expanded study follows from these results.
