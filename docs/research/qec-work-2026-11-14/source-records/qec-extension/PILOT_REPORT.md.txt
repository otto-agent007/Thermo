## What we learned

The reconstructed pipeline and saved-artifact replay work. This particular proposal/mixing budget and fallback rule are not ready to displace correlated matching. Do not scale this refiner unchanged. Tesseract and beliefmatching give promising comparator evidence on the single encountered baseline failure, but0 errors out of192 tiny heterogeneous cases does not establish general superiority or optimality. Their approximation labels remain unchanged.

Noisy d5 class-mass accuracy remains unvalidated. Next work should diagnose this known harmful development case and improve proposal/mode-coverage validation on tractable exact fixtures before requesting any broader efficacy run. Retuning on these cases would make them development data and cannot be presented as new held-out evidence.
