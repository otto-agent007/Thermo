## Interpretation

No statistical superiority or inferiority is established by192 development cases and one paired disagreement per comparison. Per-cell exact intervals remain broad; zero observed errors does not imply zero risk. The nominal pooled counts are descriptive across six different cells, not an estimate of a single homogeneous operating condition.

The pilot provides no demonstrated practical advantage for this AIS refiner. High fallback and the additional harmful correction argue against scaling this frozen configuration as-is. Tesseract/BM are useful approximate comparators here, not oracles; their one repair does not establish general superiority. Any further study needs a separately approved plan, with proposal/mixing diagnostics and stronger small-case calibration before efficacy claims.
