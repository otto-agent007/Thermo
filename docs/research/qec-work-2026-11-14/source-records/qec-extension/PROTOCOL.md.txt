# Fixed d5 technical feasibility pilot (owner review required)

## Question and limits
Can the corrected affine-coset AIS implementation execute reproducibly on archived noisy d5 syndromes, maintain usable diagnostics, and expose paired repairs/harms against correlated matching? This is a 192-case technical pilot, not a powered superiority test or a measurement of exact-MLD headroom. No claim of practical advantage, real-time latency, thermodynamic energy advantage, or convergence follows from passing.

Reuse the existing six d5 five-round phenomenological-memory cells (X/Z, p=0.005,0.01,0.02). No new shots. Select indices 0 through 31 in each 50000-shot archived cell, without reading individual outcomes to choose indices. These are development data, not a new held-out evaluation. The existing aggregate results are known. With only32 cases per cell, rare-error effects are likely unresolved, including zero observed failures. All192 selected cases remain denominators; no postselection on ESS, syndrome or correctness. No sweep, pilot expansion or parameter tuning is automatic.

## Algorithms
Baselines: saved ordinary and correlated PyMatching outputs. Established approximate comparators: Tesseract det_beam5/pqlimit200000, and beliefmatching20 product_sum iterations, pinned in requirements.lock. Tesseract approximates the most likely fault configuration, NOT degenerate logical-class mass. Neither is an oracle. Setup and decode time both count.

Refinement: parse each entire DEM error instruction as ONE independent Bernoulli mechanism, XORing repeated detector/logical targets; ^ separators do not split the fault. Gaussian elimination with stable decreasing-probability pivots gives full-rank affine coordinates for each detector+logical class. Target is the full independent DEM fault probability, including constant terms. Base is normalized independent Bernoulli over free coordinates with logit(q)=2logit(p_free); Z_base=1. This is a proposal, not a claim that conditioning preserves independence.

Per class and case: two independently seeded32-particle AIS repetitions,32 linear temperature increments,8 random-coordinate symmetric Metropolis proposals at each temperature. Ratios are accumulated BEFORE the invariant transition. The shared coordinate schedule couples particle updates; ESS is a heuristic warning only. Class decisions agree in both repetitions AND every class/repetition ESS must be at least8/32. Otherwise retain correlated matching and count fallback in the all-case endpoint. Ties choose logical0. High ESS or repeat agreement does not certify mode coverage; undetected bias remains a major risk.

## Reporting and reproducibility
Save every selected syndrome, observation, method prediction, source index, seed, per-particle logweight, class logmass and ESS; paired repairs/harms vs correlated, absolute errors/N for all methods, fallback count, timings incl proposal setup, total CPU/wall and known approximation limitations. Independent replay must verify frozen input hashes, saved-selection identity, decoder outputs and counts. Report exact marginal95% binomial intervals and paired counts; no superiority declaration based on this pilot. All-shot denominators refer to selected pilot cases, not all300000 original shots. Never extrapolate this tiny pilot's risk estimate to the full archive without uncertainty.

## Budget and authorization
One CPU process, one native numerical thread,1800 CPU seconds and4GiB address-space cap. No paid compute, no hardware, no resampling. Any error/cap stops, retains partial artifacts and reports incomplete; no dropping failures or raising caps. Owner approval must explicitly bind to manifest request SHA256 before pilot.py runs. Structural algebra, tiny synthetic checks and deterministic single-mechanism validation do not count as noisy d5 efficacy. Completion requires a separate replay result, not merely runner exit. No extension to all300000 shots without a new plan and approval.
