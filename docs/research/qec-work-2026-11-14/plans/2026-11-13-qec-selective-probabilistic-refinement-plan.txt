# QEC selective probabilistic refinement: research plan

2026-11-13

**Status: owner-selected planning work; held, not a frozen protocol.** Otto asked to
queue these two ideas and create research plans. This document authorizes neither
a probe nor a recorded run. No experiments or hardware purchases accompany it.
Before unholding, the owner reviews the exact manifest, source versions, numeric
thresholds, code, resource cap and stop rules through the existing research loop.

## Question and primary decision

Does calibrated **expected refinement benefit**, rather than failure confidence
alone, allocate a bounded probabilistic decoder more effectively than existing
confidence routing at matched full-pipeline cost? Routing hard syndromes is not
new. The possible contribution is a transferable benefit-per-cost gate with an
explicit no-benefit fallback and honest stopping calibration.

Primary metric: full-population logical error probability at a fixed mean
operation budget, including fast decoding, routing and refinement. Compare
against the strongest budget-feasible deterministic baseline and existing
confidence routing, not only a deliberately weak fast decoder. Require at least
10% relative reduction in *excess risk above exact MLD* over the best comparator,
with a paired 95% interval excluding zero improvement on the aggregate held set
and no held noise instance worsened by more than 0.001 absolute risk. These are
proposed scientific resolution thresholds, not deployment requirements. Report
absolute risk too; if comparator excess risk is zero, there is no improvement
headroom and the relative metric is undefined, not infinite or a success.

## Shared benchmark, independent question

Use the exact distance-3, single-sector, code-capacity surface-code manifest,
noise masks, development/held split and tie policy in the
[rounding plan](2026-11-13-qec-decision-aware-rounding-plan.md).
Freeze full-precision coefficients for every arm: no quantization in this row.
All syndrome probabilities and both class masses are known from enumeration,
allowing full-population risk without conditioning on selected errors. Validation
must precede performance work. This tiny family is a mechanism falsifier, not a
credible demonstration of useful runtime advantage over cheap enumeration.
Exact enumeration is expected to be highly competitive at this size. Distinguish
a correct/calibrated estimator or gate mechanism from a useful-cost pass: the
former alone cannot satisfy the primary superiority endpoint. Dominance here
closes this tiny-family cost claim, not every larger-code research possibility.
Circuit-level surface codes and qLDPC/BP are separately approved extensions,
not silently mixed into this row. Baselines must change appropriately for them.

## Stage A: establish repairable headroom before learning a gate

Fast decoder: pinned PyMatching minimum-weight decoding on the graphlike model,
with documented boundary handling and correction-to-logical-class conversion.
Compare its decision to exact degenerate MLD for every syndrome. Strong controls
include exact enumeration (reference and actual runtime comparator) and a
fixed bounded deterministic coset summation/postprocessor, ordered by candidate
weight with a frozen tie rule. Also assess the applicable Bethe/class-score
approximation identified in the certified-decoding paper before calling this
baseline set adequate. If an implementation is unavailable, report the missing
baseline and block a superiority claim rather than quietly omit it.

Run all-refine as a counterfactual first. A fast decoder's failure probability is
not expected *repair benefit*: a refiner can repeat or reverse a correct decision.
If refinement cannot improve full-population risk within the budget, stop. Do
not manufacture headroom by degrading the fast decoder or selecting hard shots.

## Stage B: bounded probabilistic refinement

Proposed refiner: annealed importance sampling (AIS) of each logical coset's
partition function. At beta=0 draw uniform stabilizer-generator coefficients,
whose independent basis yields a uniform state in that coset and known initial
partition function. Anneal to the target coefficient energy, using single-generator
Metropolis flips that leave each intermediate distribution invariant. Validate
irreducibility on the tiny coset and state-count normalization. Define
E(e)=sum_i w_i e_i including all class-dependent offsets. For r independent
stabilizer generators, estimate Z_l = 2^r * mean(exp(log_weight_l)), evaluating
the mean with log-sum-exp minus log N. The common product_i(1-p_i) cancels
between classes. Do not average log weights, self-normalize weights separately
inside each class, or drop class-dependent offsets. Freeze the order
of weight update and transition, beta schedule and energy convention in tests.

Development grid only: 16 or 64 linearly spaced annealing transitions, one full
random-permutation generator sweep per transition, and 8, 32 or 128 independent
trajectory pairs. Choose one fixed-work configuration before held evaluation.
Use log weights and retain individual trajectories. Estimate class mass ratios;
never equate a high single-trajectory weight or effective-sample-size heuristic
with a certified error bound. Compare independent class randomness against
paired common random numbers at identical marginal transitions and work. Common
random numbers can hurt: measure the variance, do not assume improvement.

Initial primary study uses fixed batches, not repeated confidence peeking.
Bootstrap entire independent trajectory pairs (or independent run replicates),
never successive Markov states. Heavy-tailed weights can make asymptotic intervals
unreliable, so check coverage against exact class probabilities on development
and held instances and show failures. No certification or ML-optimality guarantee
is claimed. Sequential stopping is a possible follow-up only after an anytime-valid
bound or separately validated finite-look policy is frozen; a bootstrap threshold
at repeatedly inspected times is not a valid certificate.

## Stage C: gate ablations and cost matching

Inputs available at inference may include fast-decoder weight, syndrome weight,
runner-up approximate class-score gap, and disagreement with the deterministic
postprocessor. No true error pattern, exact held class posterior, or future
refiner output may enter a gate. Freeze how each feature is computed and charge
its cost, including any second decoder. Train a small table or linear
calibrated gate on development cases only; no flexible model search.

Compare fast-only, all-refine, random routing, failure-risk/confidence routing,
existing decoder-disagreement routing, and proposed expected net-benefit routing.
Estimate development benefit from paired full-pipeline decisions, including both
repairs and newly broken decisions; fit expected risk reduction per measured
operation cost. Gate to refinement only when estimated benefit is positive and
the chosen threshold meets the budget. Freeze feature normalization, model,
threshold and tie policy before held evaluation. A failure-risk-only gate is an
ablation, not a substitute for the benefit gate.

Before choosing a budget, freeze a scalar work unit: one energy-term evaluation
equivalent, with integer, comparison, matching and feature-extraction work
converted using development-only single-worker microtimings. Retain the raw
operation vector and measured CPU time so this conversion can be audited; a
conclusion that reverses under actual full-pipeline timing is not a cost pass.
Choose the primary budget on development as fast-only cost plus 25% of the
all-refine incremental mean work, including gate overhead. Match random
and confidence controls to this same budget; also show curves at 10% and 50% as
secondary endpoints. Held budget overruns are failures of the budget criterion,
not permission to retune on held results. Count generator proposals, energy
terms evaluated, class-score work, feature extraction, reads/writes and host
transfers explicitly; operations are not all interchangeable. Report measured
single-worker CPU time alongside the operation vector. No projection of this
software algorithm onto physical energy without a separate mapped-hardware row.

## Analysis and rejection rules

Compute population risk by summing exact P(s) times the empirical conditional
risk of the randomized decoder, using the *same independent replicate seeds*
across matched arms. Freeze 32 independent complete-run seeds; preserve seeds
for compiler, gate fitting and trajectories separately. Confidence intervals
resample complete paired runs, weighted over the full syndrome population.
Report all noise instances, aggregate using equal instance weights, and separate
rare-syndrome strata without using them as the headline population. No rare-event
or low-logical-error claims from an underpowered sample.

Report repairs, breaks, net improvement, fraction refined, calibration curves,
mean/p95/p99 population-weighted latency, timeout rate, memory and throughput.
Measure tail timing separately from nominal operation counts, with warmup and
batching declared; the tiny-code result is not a real-time hardware claim.
Price preprocessing/compilation/training both separately and amortized over
explicit 1, 1,000 and 1,000,000-syndrome workloads.

Reject if all-refine lacks headroom; if the gate loses to matched random or
confidence routing; if improvements require exact held labels; if overhead erases
the cost benefit; if the stronger deterministic comparator dominates; or if
coverage fails when a confidence claim is needed. A positive is evidence for this
bounded allocation mechanism only, not newness of selective decoding or useful
QEC advantage. Owner review decides any code-family extension.

## Prior art that constrains the contribution

- [Adaptive decoding of quantum LDPC codes through decoder disagreement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37629):
  BP/OSD disagreement routing, including matched-budget/random controls on a
  circuit-level bivariate-bicycle code. Reimplement applicable routing concepts;
  do not transfer its qLDPC conclusions to surface-code matching by assertion.
- [Certified decoding of quantum LDPC codes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25545):
  coset partition functions, AIS and common random numbers already exist.
  Statistical confidence, exact guarantees and actual decoding success differ.
- [Adaptive Window Decoding based on Spatiotemporal Complementary Gap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14637): confidence-triggered
  buffer expansion is existing adaptive resource allocation, not this refiner.
- [PyMatching](https://pymatching.readthedocs.io/en/stable/),
  [LDPC](https://software.roffe.eu/ldpc/) and
  [Stim](https://github.com/quantumlib/Stim): baseline/simulation tooling. Only
  PyMatching is proposed for the first graphlike case; BP+OSD/LSD and circuit
  simulation belong to later appropriately scoped families.

All external performance claims remain `asserted`, not Thermo reproductions.
Before a protocol, pin paper versions and source cards, audit released code,
clarify missing comparisons and narrow or reject the novelty claim accordingly.

## Follow-up: explicit net-benefit target and calibration boundary

Record four paired outcomes: both right, repaired (fast wrong/refined right),
harmed (fast right/refined wrong), and both wrong. The target is
[P(repaired given features) - P(harmed given features)] divided by the conditional expected positive
incremental refinement cost given those same features. For variable-cost refinement,
estimate that expectation from fitting data; do not average noisy per-shot
benefit/cost ratios. Define zero-incremental-cost cases separately rather than
dividing by zero. Charge gate overhead in the complete pipeline.
This is a proposed objective, not an established transferable algorithm. Labels
may use simulated ground truth for fitting, never at inference. Tiny exact
posteriors can integrate outcomes for analysis but remain oracle-only information.

Partition development instances into disjoint fitting and calibration groups,
with literal membership frozen before fitting. Use fitting data for the gate and
calibration data for the threshold; held instances stay untouched. Do not impose
monotonicity in failure risk on net benefit: use the table or linear option above,
not a monotonic failure-risk assumption. Freeze timeout and fallback rules. Return the
fast answer on timeout or invalid estimates, charging all incurred cost. Include
all four outcomes, failures and fallbacks in the same all-shot denominator.

## Follow-up: separately gated noise-model mismatch stress test

Keep the initial independent-noise study unchanged. Only after its headroom and
fixed-work mechanism checks, propose an owner-reviewed mismatch manifest:
stationary matched control, spatial bias, correlated/burst errors and gradual
drift, reported separately. Define P_true(error) independently of P_assumed(error).
All risk, repairability and headroom are evaluated under P_true, not the decoder's
assumed model. For correlated noise enumerate a normalized joint distribution
over all 512 errors, such as a frozen latent mixture; a product of marginals is
not an exact reference. If that contract cannot be validated, defer the arm.

Use chronological fitting/calibration/test splits for drift, with no future
information in a gate and independent simulation seeds. Compare gap-only against
gap plus model-compatibility features (e.g. normalized minimum matching weight),
and against the existing disagreement gate. Test prediction of net refinability,
not just failure risk. A refiner sharing a wrong model can confidently increase
error; distinguish compute-only refinement from model adaptation. No automatic
retraining is included. Keep every shot in the primary denominator: compatibility
research based on postselection does not establish an all-shot refinement benefit.
Report calibration shift, repairs, harms and budget overruns per mismatch family.
This extension needs separate sizes, thresholds, caps and approval; it is outside
the initial run budget, not permission to expand after a negative result.

## Additional prior art and reuse checks

- [Confidence-gated hybrid decoding, July 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05814v2):
  neural fast path with matching fallback constrains the confidence-routing
  novelty claim; batched CPU throughput is not demonstrated real-time hardware.
- [Noise-model compatibility, September 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29018v1):
  motivates compatibility features under drift/correlation; its intervention is
  postselection, not the all-shot refinement objective proposed here.
- [PriorTilt, November 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13373):
  sparse BP-prior perturbation is a candidate baseline for a later qLDPC study,
  not another arm for this surface-code study. Released implementation and
  independent reproduction must be checked before relying on performance claims.
- [decoder-bench](https://github.com/satvikmaurya/decoder-bench) and
  [Sinter](https://github.com/quantumlib/Stim/blob/main/glue/sample/README.md):
  assess reusable syndrome/observable formats before building infrastructure.
  Pin versions, data hashes, conventions and licenses; compare identical held
  shots. Circuit-level datasets do not replace this code-capacity benchmark.

These are research leads, not Thermo reproductions or exhaustive novelty review.
