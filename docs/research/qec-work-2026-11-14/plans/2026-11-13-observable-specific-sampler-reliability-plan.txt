# Observable-specific sampler reliability: candidate research plan

2026-11-13

**Status: owner-selected planning work; held, not a frozen protocol.** This adds
one foundational candidate, not a runnable experiment. No existing stopped study,
archive or scheduler changes. Protocol, exact manifest, thresholds and resource
caps require owner review before any probe or recorded study.

## Decision and hypothesis

Can an affordable vector of observable-specific diagnostics predict which
estimates fail a fixed accuracy tolerance on unseen binary models better than
coefficient MSE or a single global convergence statistic? The design number is
the false-safe rate at a fixed useful acceptance coverage: which reported
observables can be trusted without exact target information at inference?

Proposed primary resolution: normalized absolute error at most 0.02; at least
50% of held model/observable cases accepted; false-safe rate among accepted cases
at most 5%, with a one-sided 95% upper interval at most 10%. Compare risk-coverage
curves and calibration against baselines at matched diagnostic cost. These are
planning choices for review, not scientifically established tolerances. Do not
claim reliability from a point estimate when the interval is unresolved.

Hypothesis: low-bit representation and finite mixing produce distinguishable
observable error patterns that generalize beyond development instances. A useful
negative is a counterexample to a universal diagnostic, not a reason to relax the
tolerance. A successful calibrated predictor is not an exact certificate.

## Bounded first family and exact decomposition

Propose 12-spin open 3x4 nearest-neighbour Ising models, with 4096 states each.
Freeze explicit fields/couplings and dimensionless energy conventions. Include
ferromagnetic, weak-field and frustrated instances, but hold out entire model
instances, not correlated states. Select 12 fitting, 12 calibration and 24 held
instances, balanced across the three strata, before outcomes. This is an initial
within-family transfer test, not evidence of transfer to larger graphs or tasks.
No archived denoising or adsorption model is reopened.

Use exact enumeration for original and rounded equilibrium references. Propose
full precision and 4/6/8-bit nearest rounding under one declared signed codebook
and cap; no cap optimization on held results. One single-site Gibbs kernel only,
with fixed random-scan convention, independent starts and sweep budgets 4/16/64.
No new sampler portfolio, learning pipeline, hardware mapping or energy claim.
Exact finite-time propagation is allowed only if sparse transitions and all
requested cases fit the separately approved probe cap; never construct an
unbounded dense transition matrix. Otherwise report finite-time bias with
replicated uncertainty, not as an exact decomposition.

For each observable f, distinguish:
- representation bias: E_pi_rounded[f] - E_pi_original[f];
- finite-time bias at original coefficients: E_mu_t_original[f] - E_pi_original[f];
- interaction: the difference between finite-time bias at rounded and original
  coefficients, each measured relative to its own equilibrium;
- sampling variance and autocorrelation around the corresponding estimator mean.

The first three sum to total mean error relative to the original target. MSE
additionally includes variance. Exact equilibrium and propagated distributions
are `exact_reference`; sampled trajectories are `software_simulation`.

Predeclare mean magnetization, susceptibility, nearest-neighbour correlation,
domain-wall density and one local spin expectation as the task observable.
Specify normalization, estimator and tolerance for each before execution.
Susceptibility is a function of first/second moments, not a single raw-state
observable: account for finite-sample plug-in bias separately. Domain walls and
correlation are related; do not count them as independent replications. Avoid
claiming a constraint-validity endpoint without actual constraints in this model.

## Permitted information and controls

Operational candidates may use replicated-start disagreement, blockwise estimate
stability, observable-specific autocorrelation/ESS and between-chain diagnostics.
Freeze a small feature set and simple calibrated predictor on fitting data, then
choose thresholds on calibration data only. Include estimator history and all
extra chains in diagnostic cost. A sample is one retained full spin state; an
independently seeded run is the uncertainty unit, not each correlated state.
Propose 32 independent run seeds, grouped by held model for performance intervals.

Baselines: coefficient MSE, one predeclared scalar diagnostic, and observable-wise
standard diagnostics without the combined predictor. Match total updates, reads
and diagnostic processing. Exact target moments, total-variation errors and
original-versus-rounded equilibrium discrepancies are oracle explanatory
features only. Report their predictive ceiling separately, never as deployable
features. Each feature table must state permitted inputs and computational cost.
Global importance-weight ESS and MCMC autocorrelation ESS are different quantities;
this study must not use criticism of one as a general invalidation of the other.

The primary error endpoint is the absolute error of each independent run's
reported estimator against the exact original target moment, not the error of
its expectation over runs. A false-safe event is an accepted run/observable
estimate whose normalized error exceeds 0.02; coverage counts accepted
run/observable estimates among all eligible held estimates. The bias decomposition
above concerns estimator means across runs and is a separate mechanism analysis,
not a substitute for this realized-error endpoint. Weight held models equally,
then observable/budget/precision cases and runs equally within each model.
Resample whole model instances with all observables/runs together for uncertainty.
Freeze interval construction and risk/coverage aggregation in the protocol. Report
each stratum and observable, not only pooled averages. If 24 held models cannot
resolve the criterion, report inconclusive rather than add samples automatically.

## Falsifiers, stages and caps

Stop if the combined diagnostic loses to the cheapest standard diagnostic at
matched cost, fails held calibration/coverage, or needs exact target information
to work. Also stop if there is no failure headroom or diagnostics cost essentially
as much as exact solution. No larger graph or relaxed tolerance follows by default.

1. Audit prior art and existing Thermo diagnostics; reuse helpers without editing
   hash-bound sources. Freeze literal model arrays, observable definitions,
   codebook, split, estimators and diagnostic costs before a probe.
2. Separately approved feasibility probe: CPU only, one worker, 30 CPU-minutes,
   4 GiB memory, 1 GiB artifacts. Check exact references and propagation feasibility.
3. Owner-reviewed frozen protocol and tests before a recorded study. Proposed cap:
   two CPU-hours, one worker, 4 GiB memory, 2 GiB artifacts. Runs over 30 minutes
   require tested autosave/resume. Cap exhaustion means incomplete, not expansion.
4. Deliver error decomposition, risk-coverage/calibration tables, cost accounting,
   counterexamples, hashes and replay. No physical-hardware conclusion.

## Prior art and novelty boundary

- [Evaluating generative samplers for lattice field theory by scale](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11108v1)
  motivates resolving errors by observable/scale rather than one global overlap
  statistic. It is a lattice-field/importance-weight result, not validation of
  this binary-model predictor. Its marginal correction needs target information;
  finite local relaxation can retain bias. No free target-free certificate follows.
- Existing observable-specific MCMC ESS, Monte Carlo standard errors, replicated
  chains and convergence diagnostics are mandatory controls, not new inventions.
  Before freezing, pin primary references and implementation conventions for
  these diagnostics and assess what they already solve.
- [Thermo rounding-transfer probe](2026-11-05-rounding-rule-transfer-probe.md)
  motivates observable dependence but does not establish the proposed predictor.
- [Time-varying rounding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22621) and
  [perfect sampling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37300) are future alternatives to
  audit, not extra arms. Averaging rounded kernels need not preserve the target
  Gibbs law; perfect-sampling guarantees apply only in their stated regimes.

External findings are `asserted`, not Thermo reproductions. Novelty remains open:
first compare against standard observable-resolved diagnostics, then ask whether
separating quantization and finite-time errors adds predictive value. No claim of
new sampler, computational advantage or general certificate is made here.
