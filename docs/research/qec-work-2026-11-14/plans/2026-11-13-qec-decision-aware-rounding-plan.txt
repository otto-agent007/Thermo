# QEC decision-aware coefficient rounding: research plan

2026-11-13

**Status: owner-selected planning work; held, not a frozen protocol.** Otto asked to
queue these two ideas and create research plans. This document authorizes neither
a probe nor a recorded run. No experiments or hardware purchases accompany it.
Before unholding, the owner reviews the exact manifest, source versions, numeric
thresholds, code, resource cap and stop rules through the existing research loop.

## Question and decision

Can a shared, finite-precision representation preserve logical-class decisions
better than coefficient-wise nearest rounding, at the same coefficient codebook?
The output is a bit requirement on a small specified decoding family, not a QEC
threshold, hardware speedup or general-purpose rounding recommendation.

This follows the negative transfer result in
[the P-0010 probe](2026-11-05-rounding-rule-transfer-probe.md): error cancellation
that helps one observable need not protect another. Test the relevant decision
margin directly, rather than assume that mean coefficient error predicts it.

**Primary endpoint:** smallest stable bit count in 2 through 8 for which excess
logical risk over full-precision degenerate maximum-likelihood decoding is at
most 0.001 on *every* frozen held-out noise instance. Stable means all tested
higher precisions also pass; report individual nonmonotone errors, not a smoothed
curve. The tolerance is a proposed mechanism-study resolution, not an acceptable
logical error rate for a deployed quantum computer. A useful positive reduces
this requirement by at least one bit versus nearest rounding without extra
per-syndrome information. Report failures and results above the grid separately.

## Exact benchmark and leakage boundary

Start with the rotated distance-3 surface code, nine data qubits, one X-error
sector, perfect syndrome measurements and independent code-capacity noise.
Enumerate all 512 error patterns. Freeze the parity-check matrix H, logical
operator L, independent stabilizer generators, coordinate ordering and canonical
syndrome representatives in a hash-bound manifest. Check ranks and commutation.
This is two logical classes per syndrome, not a circuit-level or full Pauli study.

For error e, syndrome s and class l, compute
W(s,l) = sum over matching e of product_i p_i^e_i (1-p_i)^(1-e_i).
The Bayes decision maximizes W; the risk of any decision d is
R(d) = sum_s [sum_l W(s,l) - W(s,d(s))]. Quantized decisions are evaluated
under the *original* noise probabilities, not under their rounded surrogate.
Use log-sum-exp for class sums and verify normalization. Exact ties are co-optimal;
a deterministic smallest-index tie break supports replay, but disagreement among
co-optimal classes is not an error. Report ties and near-ties separately with a
predeclared numerical comparison tolerance and a high-precision cross-check.

Noise instances use p_i = p * (1 + 0.5 m_i), where m_i is in {-1,0,1}.
Development: p in {0.02,0.05,0.08}, homogeneous and checkerboard masks.
Held-out: p in {0.035,0.065}, homogeneous, vertical-stripe and single-central-site
high-noise masks. The central-site mask is +1 centrally and 0 elsewhere.
Checkerboard and stripes alternate +1/-1 by fixed coordinates. Commit the literal
arrays before computing results. This keeps p_i between 0.01 and 0.12. Include
homogeneous noise as a diagnostic: common coefficient scaling can preserve
minimum-energy decisions while changing degenerate class probabilities.
No tuning to held-out syndrome outcomes or selecting only ambiguous syndromes.

## Representation and proposed algorithm

Quantize noise log-odds w_i = log((1-p_i)/p_i), **not BP messages or final class
scores**. Use the same signed integer codebook in every arm: cap C=log(99),
step C/(2^(b-1)-1), integer levels from -(2^(b-1)-1) through +(2^(b-1)-1).
No clipping is expected; log any violation rather than enlarge the cap. Rounding
may yield zero. Infer all classes exactly under rounded weights, isolating
representation error from estimator error. A future physical embedding would
need a separately approved codebook/placement study; this is not the archived
constraint layer's native codebook or a claim of Z1 compatibility.

Compare:
1. nearest, with frozen midpoint policy;
2. independent unbiased stochastic rounding, 32 compiler seeds;
3. class error diffusion where equal-coefficient classes exist, frozen ordering;
4. decision-aware correlated rounding with the same floor/ceiling choices.

For arm 4, calculate development gradients g_s of each log class-weight ratio
with respect to coefficients. Form a fixed positive-semidefinite sensitivity
matrix A = mean over development instances of sum_s P(s) g_s g_s^T.
Freeze A before held evaluation. For each input calibration vector, choose the
floor/ceiling vector minimizing delta^T A delta, with lexicographic tie breaking.
There are at most 512 choices at n=9, so this compiler is bounded and transparent.
It uses calibration coefficients but never held-out exact class labels or
syndrome-specific codebooks. Charge compilation separately; exhaustive search
is not scalable novelty. The hypothesis is that shared sensitivity predicts
margin damage outside its development instances. Assess predicted versus actual
class-margin perturbation, with a coefficient-MSE matched diagnostic. An oracle
A computed on held instances can only be labelled an exploratory upper bound,
never the primary method or evidence of transfer.

## Validation, analysis and falsifiers

Before outcomes: independently implement direct error enumeration and stabilizer
coset enumeration; compare every syndrome/class mass. Test identity rounding,
normalization, syndrome correction, stabilizer-invariant logical labels, codebook
bounds and deterministic replay. A deliberately corrupted logical label must
fail validation. Use analytic derivative versus finite differences for g_s.

Report exact risk and excess risk for every instance/bit/rule, tie-aware weighted
disagreement, class-margin strata, compiler time and memory. Deterministic exact
arms need numerical error checks, not shot-noise confidence intervals. Compiler
randomness is summarized across independent seeds with intervals; no pooling
individual syndromes as independent experimental replicates. Report per-instance
and worst-case outcomes as well as aggregates. The deterministic candidate is
judged directly, not cherry-picked from random seeds.

Stop this candidate if no stable-bit reduction transfers, if it depends on oracle
held-instance sensitivities, or if improvements disappear against class diffusion
or stochastic rounding at equal representation cost. If all methods already have
zero excess risk, report no headroom, not a success. A null result does not justify
larger codes or a different tolerance without a new owner decision.

No sampling, training, beta jitter, circuit-level noise, threshold extrapolation
or energy projection in this row. A positive motivates a new larger-code protocol;
a negative closes the tested mechanism. Existing denoising and adsorption stops
remain untouched. The companion [selective-refinement plan](2026-11-13-qec-selective-probabilistic-refinement-plan.md)
can reuse verified reference code but must not conflate rounding with estimation.

## Research grounding

- [Diversity Methods for Improving Convergence and Accuracy of Quantum Error Correction Decoders Through Hardware Emulation](https://doi.org/10.22331/q-2026-04-16-2071):
  quantization diversity is existing prior art. Its message-level quantization is
  not the same object as this coefficient-level class-margin objective; include
  it in the novelty review, not as an equivalent baseline without adaptation.
- [Certified decoding of quantum LDPC codes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25545):
  class partition functions and probabilistic comparison already exist. We test
  representational damage with exact sums before approximate estimators.
- [PyMatching documentation](https://pymatching.readthedocs.io/en/stable/):
  graphlike matching is useful later; matching is not generally degenerate MLD.

External claims are `asserted`, not reproduced in Thermo. Record exact paper
versions, implementation licenses and applicable code/noise regimes before a
protocol; this plan makes no literature-exhaustiveness or novelty claim.

## Follow-up: decision-preservation analysis

Use dimensionless energies E (beta is already absorbed into log-odds). For
identical class supports, if abs(E'_l(x)-E_l(x)) <= epsilon_l for every state,
then exp(-epsilon_l) Z_l <= Z'_l <= exp(epsilon_l) Z_l. Consequently
abs(log Z'_l-log Z_l) <= epsilon_l. An original unique winner a is preserved
if log Z_a-log Z_b > epsilon_a+epsilon_b for every competitor b. This elementary
sensitivity bound is an analysis tool, not a novelty claim. Non-strict comparisons
do not resolve ties. Retain class-dependent offsets and state multiplicities;
only class-independent offsets cancel from decisions.

Compute classwise epsilon and original margins exactly in this tiny diagnostic.
Report bound tightness, class flips versus margin-normalized perturbation and
cases where the sufficient bound fails but the winner survives. Separate zero
margins rather than dividing by zero. Exact margins and classwise maxima remain
oracle diagnostics, never deployed features or evidence of transferable prediction.
A later scalable precision allocator would need affordable bounds on differences
in class-conditional sensitivity, not merely coefficient magnitude. Separate
representation, estimator, mapping and noise-model errors. This analysis adds no
sampling or new experiment family to the initial plan.

Also report the P_true(s)-weighted mass of uncertified syndromes, not just their
unweighted count. Stability relative to an assumed model is not correctness under
noise mismatch. Distinguish cheap universal coefficient bounds from exact
per-coset extrema and report their costs; worst-case bounds may be very loose.

## Mechanism pass versus practical advantage

A one-bit stable requirement reduction is representation headroom, not by itself
an application win. This plan uses exact class inference from rounded log-odds;
it does not establish that a deployed decoder computes the same decision with
finite-width accumulators, BP messages, lookup tables or matching weights.
The output is a logical-class decision, not a claim that rounding improves the
physical noise or recovers the true error pattern. Exact MLD remains the risk floor.

Before claiming practical benefit, a separately approved downstream study must
name the actual decoder and its coefficient, intermediate and output precision,
then verify the same held risk tolerance end to end. Compare against nearest,
class diffusion, stochastic rounding and the strongest practical full-precision
or alternative decoder, including coefficient calibration, sensitivity fitting,
compiler search, storage, decoding and update/recompile cost. Report cost amortized
across declared reuse counts and changes in calibration. Require a measured
resource reduction at matched risk, with no hidden increase elsewhere, rather
than infer memory, latency or energy savings from nominal bit count. If all bit
widths use the same machine word or compiler overhead dominates, there may be no
practical gain. No physical energy advantage can be claimed from this CPU study.
