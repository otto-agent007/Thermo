Ordinary matching is the stronger of these two baselines on this particular symmetric finite-code instance. Correlated matching is not guaranteed to improve every case.

This establishes a materially larger oracle repair opportunity than the earlier X-only probe. It does not show that AIS, a learned gate, or any practical decoder can recover the gap efficiently. Exact enumeration remains very cheap at d3. The matched homogeneous depolarizing, perfect-measurement family does not establish performance under noisy measurements, circuit faults, bias, drift or larger codes.
