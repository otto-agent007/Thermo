# QEC selective probabilistic refinement: feasibility result

**Completed, integrity checks passed. Mechanism works narrowly; practical advantage not established.**

The approved development-only probe completed all 2,304 cells with persisted
replay in 278.84 CPU seconds (4.65 minutes), using 83.1 MiB peak resident memory.
Independent audit verified every cell, its source/receipt hashes, 4,128,768
trajectory log weights, partition arithmetic, decisions, all-population risk
and repair-minus-harm accounting. No held data or full study was run.

## What we learned

1. **Very little headroom exists here.** PyMatching already achieves exact
   maximum-likelihood population risk in five of six development instances.
   Only the p=0.05 checkerboard instance has a repairable degeneracy error,
   at syndrome 15. Its risk can fall from 0.0401302868 to 0.0400021365:
   an absolute improvement of 0.0001281503 (about 0.32% relative).
2. **AIS can recover that correction.** At 16 transitions and 128 trajectory
   pairs, both independent-class and common-random-number variants attained
   exact population risk in all 32 observed complete-run replicates. This is
   finite observed evidence, not an assurance about every future random run.
3. **Insufficient refinement harms good answers.** At 16 transitions and eight
   pairs, mean risk worsened by roughly 0.00030 across the six instances. A
   gate must predict net repairs minus harms, not merely identify hard cases.
4. **Common random numbers help variance, not uniformly decisions.** Aggregate
   weighted log-ratio variance was lower in all tested grid settings, but the
   lowest-budget paired variant had slightly worse mean decoding risk.

Maximum possible equal-instance mean risk improvement is only 0.0000213584.
All 12 configurations and their 32-replicate ranges are in the independent
report. No confidence, coverage or generalization claim follows from selecting
the best observed development configuration.

## Decision and next step

Do not automatically launch the full gate study on this tiny family. We now have
validated estimator infrastructure and a useful repair-versus-harm example, but
not a practical-cost result. Full coset-energy tables were precomputed, so exact
summation remains cheap; matched-budget routing, deterministic postprocessing,
the relevant Bethe/class-score comparator and held evaluation remain undone.

My recommendation is to close this bounded probe as a successful mechanism
check with insufficient practical headroom, then review whether a separately
scoped benchmark offers genuine repairable headroom before spending on a full
learned-gate study. No larger code, drift test or resource expansion is approved.
