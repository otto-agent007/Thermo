# d5 noisy-measurement baseline benchmark

Evidence: software_simulation. 300000 sampled shots, six fixed cells, 50000 shots per cell. Separate logical X/Z memory observables, not joint logical-Pauli failures.

| Memory | Data/readout rate | Ordinary failures | Correlated failures | Ordinary logical risk | Correlated logical risk | Paired improvement, simultaneous 95% interval |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| X | 0.5% | 7 | 8 | 0.014% | 0.016% | -0.002 pp [-0.0271, 0.0232] |
| X | 1% | 78 | 45 | 0.156% | 0.090% | 0.066 pp [0.0049, 0.1268] |
| X | 2% | 581 | 377 | 1.162% | 0.754% | 0.408 pp [0.2576, 0.5581] |
| Z | 0.5% | 9 | 5 | 0.018% | 0.010% | 0.008 pp [-0.0151, 0.0308] |
| Z | 1% | 81 | 48 | 0.162% | 0.096% | 0.066 pp [0.0074, 0.1243] |
| Z | 2% | 596 | 447 | 1.192% | 0.894% | 0.298 pp [0.1437, 0.4522] |

Correlated matching improves on ordinary matching at 1% and 2% in both memories, with all four paired simultaneous confidence intervals excluding zero. At 0.5%, the few observed failures leave the difference unresolved; the X-memory point estimate is slightly worse. No cell is dropped or added.

At 1% noise, observed relative failure reductions are 42.3% (X) and 40.7% (Z); at 2%, 35.1% (X) and 25.0% (Z). These relative point estimates are not uncertainty-free. The intervals above are absolute paired improvements and use the predeclared conservative familywise 95% union-bound method. Individual risk intervals, repair/harm counts and full raw decisions are in results.json and NPZ files.

This stronger baseline matters: the earlier d3 perfect-measurement test favored ordinary matching, but that ordering did not transfer to d5 repeated noisy measurements. Neither experiment establishes that a probabilistic refiner would improve correlated matching at acceptable cost.

Residual headroom to optimal decoding remains UNKNOWN: this study has no exact or independently validated stronger approximate reference. No stronger-reference, sampler, learned-gate or additional noise study was launched. A reasonable next separately reviewed research step is validating a stronger class-likelihood reference before investing in refinement.

The physical model is independent data depolarization before each of five rounds and independent ancilla-record flips, with ideal gates, resets and final data readout. It is not circuit-level noise or real hardware. X/Z trials use independent fixed seeds and are separate component-memory measurements.

Recorded runner computation: 1.7155 CPU seconds, 1.7176 wall seconds, peak RSS 154240 KiB (~150.6 MiB). Includes sampling, decoder construction/calls, metric calculation and most artifacts; excludes process startup/imports and subsequent independent audit. Batch decoder timings are not per-shot tail latency, energy, hardware measurements or a real-time guarantee.

Approval is recorded separately against the immutable requested-input SHA256. Original requested labels remain unchanged; observed execution state is in results.json and independent completion.json.
