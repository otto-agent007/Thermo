# QEC decision-aware coefficient rounding: completed study

## Conclusion

**Negative on the frozen mechanism criterion.** Decision-aware rounding requires 3 stable bits, exactly the same as ordinary nearest rounding. It therefore does not deliver the required one-bit reduction. Stop this candidate under the approved rule; no larger-code study, tolerance change or new hardware follows automatically.

It beats diffusion and expected stochastic rounding on this tiny family, but beating weaker alternatives is not the required advantage over nearest. This is not evidence of practical latency, memory or energy savings.

## Held-out result

| Rule | Smallest stable bits | Worst excess at 2 bits | Worst excess at 3 bits |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| nearest | 3 | 0.123078893 | 0.000000000 |
| decision | 3 | 0.144113445 | 0.000000000 |
| diffusion | 4 | 0.123078893 | 0.035727870 |
| stochastic_expected | 4 | 0.108048523 | 0.031357791 |

Stable means excess logical risk at most 0.001 on every held-out instance at that precision and every higher tested precision through 8 bits. At 3 bits, both nearest and decision-aware have zero excess risk on every held case. At 2 bits, decision-aware is worse in worst-case excess risk (0.144113445 vs 0.123078893). Tiny stochastic excess values around 7e-18 are floating-point summation residuals, not physical risk claims.

## Design and separation

Rotated distance 3 surface code,9 data qubits, X sector, perfect syndrome measurements, all 512 physical error patterns. Development: 6 noise cases at base rates .02,.05,.08, homogeneous/checkerboard masks. Held: 6 untouched cases at .035,.065, homogeneous/vertical-stripe/central masks. All 7 precisions 2–8 were evaluated,42 cells per stage. The single sensitivity matrix was fit only on development cases; identical sensitivity hashes appear in both receipts.

Nearest, class diffusion, decision-aware floor/ceiling search and independent stochastic rounding share the same signed codebook capped at ln99. Stochastic comparison uses exact expectation over rounding vectors;32 fixed seeds describe variability, not best-seed selection. Quantized class decisions preserve common-step integer energies and are audited at 70 and 100 decimal digits. True-noise risks use rational probability products. Assumed-model bound certificates are explicitly separate from true-noise correctness.
